Metro Mayhem Rules - v1 - July 14th, 2026

1 - How It Works

1.1 - Complete as many tasks as possible within the event time limit.
1.2 - Unless stated otherwise, tasks may be completed in any order.
1.3 - You must reach the finish line before the event cut-off time to receive a result.

2 - Travel

2.1 - Travel using the transport network specified for the event and on foot.
2.2 - Tasks are generally located close to stations or stops on the specified transport network.
2.3 - Private transport, bikes, taxis, ride-hailing services and personal vehicles are not allowed.

3 - Evidence
This is a more casual event that our longer races and as such invigilation takes a more light touch approach.

3.1 - For the winner we will invigilate based on their checkpoint checkin hisotry and task photos.
3.2 - For everyone else, we'll check you've hit the minimum points required in our app.

4 - Conduct

4.1 - Play fairly, obey all laws and transport operator rules, and follow organiser instructions.
4.2 - Treat fellow racers, organisers and members of the public with respect.

5 - Race Control

5.1 - Results are provisional until invigilation has been completed.
5.2 - Race Control may apply points deductions, task invalidation, time penalties or disqualification where necessary.
5.3 - Where these rules do not cover a situation, Race Control will decide based on fairness, safety and the spirit of the event. Their decision is final.
